## Purpose
Make the released product findable and understandable — store listings, keywords, launch content.

## When to use
- A release exists or is scheduled.
- Store listing, screenshots, keywords or launch content need updating.

## Operating rules
- Positioning first: who the app is for, the one job it does better, and the proof. Reuse this sentence everywhere.
- Keyword research per store and locale; track search volume, difficulty and current rank.
- Store copy written natively per locale, not translated word for word.
- Screenshots with captions that state the benefit; preview video follows the design system.
- In-app review prompt at a success moment; reply routine assigned.
- Measure impressions, page views, conversion, installs and retention by source; iterate the listing like a product journey.
