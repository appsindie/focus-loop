# Go-to-Market and ASO

Make a released product findable and understandable. Follow the `go-to-market` skill.

This is Phase 4's *findability* job. Deciding whether the product keeps its portfolio slot is `!grow_product` — a different question with a different gate.

Entry condition: a release exists or is scheduled.

## Actions

1. **Positioning first**: who it is for, the one job it does better, the proof. Everything else derives from that sentence.
2. **Keyword research** per store and per locale (Vietnamese first for the VN market): volume, difficulty, current rank — record the baseline.
3. **Listing**: title, subtitle, keyword field, short and long description, what's-new — written natively per locale, not translated word for word.
4. **Creative**: icon, screenshots with benefit captions, preview video, all following the product's design system.
5. **Ratings**: in-app prompt at a moment of success, plus a routine for replying to reviews.
6. **Measure**: impressions -> product page views -> conversion -> installs -> retention by source. Iterate on the listing like a product journey.

## Report back

The positioning sentence, the keyword baseline, what went live per locale, and the conversion numbers to watch.
